Our Journey

From a Vision to India's Top Agency

Fitscalez was born from a simple yet powerful idea — that every Indian business deserves a world-class digital presence. Founded in Delhi in 2021 by Mr. Hariom Saini, we started with a small team and a big dream: to help Indian businesses compete globally through technology and smart marketing.

Today, Fitscalez stands as one of Delhi's most trusted digital agencies. Our co-founder Ms. Anshul Sharma drives our marketing strategy, while our expert development team led by Divyansh (Contact: +91 9599433616) delivers cutting-edge web solutions that truly make a difference.

We've helped 80+ businesses across PAN-India achieve measurable growth — from cutting-edge tech platforms like Walk The Plan (1:1 Scale Blueprint Projection) to luxury architecture studios like House of Symmetry — with our comprehensive digital services including MERN Stack development, SEO, social media marketing, and business strategy.
