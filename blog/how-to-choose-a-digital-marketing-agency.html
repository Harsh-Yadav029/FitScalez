How to Choose a Digital Marketing Agency in Delhi NCR: A Founder's Guide

Tired of agencies reporting impressions and reach while your bank account remains flat? Here is how to evaluate digital marketing partners based on ROAS and pipeline growth.

A
Anshul Sharma
Partner & Strategy Director • Fitscalez
Share: 💬 💼 🐦

Delhi NCR is home to thousands of digital agencies, but business owners frequently complain about the same frustrating experience: "We paid an agency for 6 months, got pretty PDF reports full of impressions and impressions, but zero actual sales."

A legitimate marketing partner should function as an extension of your sales team, holding themselves accountable to Cost per Acquisition (CPA), Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), and bottom-line revenue.

The Trap of Vanity Metrics

Never judge marketing success by likes, follower counts, or raw impressions. These metrics cannot pay your team's salaries. Focus exclusively on conversion metrics: qualified leads generated, cost per qualified lead, customer acquisition cost, and revenue generated.
