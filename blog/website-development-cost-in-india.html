How Much Does Website Development Cost in India? (2025-2026 Price Guide)

A transparent, comprehensive guide to website pricing in India. We break down the real costs of domain, hosting, UI/UX design, custom MERN stack engineering, and ongoing maintenance.

One of the most frequent questions Indian business owners, startup founders, and enterprise managers ask is: "How much does a professional website cost in India?"

The answer is often frustratingly vague: "It depends." A simple template site can cost as little as ₹10,000, while a custom, secure full-stack web application can range from ₹80,000 to ₹5,00,000 or more. In this comprehensive guide, we provide an honest, transparent breakdown based on real market rates across India.

💡 Key Takeaway for 2025-2026

For most Indian SMEs and service businesses, a high-performance, mobile-optimized custom website starts between ₹25,000 and ₹45,000. Custom web applications with user portals, booking engines, or dashboards start from ₹49,000 to ₹1,50,000+.

To help you benchmark your budget, here is the realistic price spectrum in the Indian market:

Pricing Breakdown by Website Category

1. Static Business Websites (₹15,000 – ₹30,000)

A static website consists of 5 to 8 informational pages (Home, About Us, Services, Portfolio, Contact, Terms). Built using modern HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript, these websites load in milliseconds, have virtually zero security vulnerabilities, and require very little server maintenance.

2. Dynamic Corporate Websites (₹30,000 – ₹60,000)

These websites include a dynamic content management system (CMS) or admin panel allowing non-technical team members to publish blogs, update case studies, edit team members, and view incoming leads in real time. They often include interactive calculators, appointment scheduling, and WhatsApp API integration.

3. E-Commerce Platforms (₹35,000 – ₹1,20,000)

Selling products online requires product catalog management, shopping cart functionality, secure payment gateways (Razorpay, Cashfree, Stripe, PayU), automated shipping calculator integration (Shiprocket), customer accounts, and automated email/SMS order notifications.

4. Custom MERN Stack Applications (₹50,000 – ₹2,50,000+)

When off-the-shelf templates cannot support your workflow, bespoke software built with MongoDB, Express.js, React.js, and Node.js (MERN) provides unlimited flexibility, robust database scalability, and lightning-fast user experiences.

Key Factors Driving Development Costs

  • UI/UX Design Quality: Custom Figma design systems tailored to your brand identity require significantly more craft than pre-made WordPress themes.
  • Technology Stack: Custom React/Node.js architecture offers unmatched speed and security compared to bloated page builders.
  • Number of Unique Page Templates: A 10-page site with unique layouts costs more than a 20-page site repeating the same blog template.
  • Third-Party Integrations: CRM connections (HubSpot, Zoho), payment gateways (Razorpay), ERP hooks, and automated WhatsApp APIs add engineering hours.
  • SEO Foundation: High-ranking websites require semantic HTML, JSON-LD Schema markup, optimized Core Web Vitals, and clean sitemaps built right into the code.

Ongoing & Hidden Costs You Must Budget For

Building the website is only the initial step. Factor in these mandatory operational costs:

  • Domain Registration: ₹800 – ₹1,200 per year (.com, .in, .co.in).
  • Hosting / VPS Server: ₹3,000 – ₹15,000 per year depending on traffic (Hostinger, AWS, DigitalOcean, Hetzner).
  • SSL Certificate: Free via Let's Encrypt or ₹2,000 – ₹8,000/year for enterprise wildcards.
  • Maintenance & Security Updates: ₹2,000 – ₹6,000/month for backups, security patches, uptime monitoring, and small content tweaks.
